Privacy Policy

Cadence CRM · Last updated 27 September 2026

Cadence CRM ("Cadence", "we", "us") is a customer relationship management app at app.pultly.com, run by Simplicity Labs, Južni bulevar 83, Belgrade, Serbia. This policy explains what personal data we handle when you use Cadence, why, who helps us run it, and the rights you have. Questions: alex@simplicity-labs.com.

Two kinds of data

  • Your account. Data about you as a user: your sign-in, name, email and workspace memberships. For this we are the controller.
  • What you put into your workspace. Companies, contacts, deals, notes, products, to-dos and documents that you and your team enter or import. This data belongs to the organisation that owns the workspace. It is the controller; we store and process it only on its behalf and on its instructions, as its processor. People listed as contacts in a workspace should contact that organisation about their data. We will pass on any request we receive.

What we collect

DataWhat it isWhy
Sign-in An identifier from our sign-in provider (Auth0), your email address and name. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with the sign-in provider; Cadence keeps only your name and email. Error reports When something breaks, a technical report: the error, the page or request, browser type and app version, and your internal user id. No names, email addresses, cookies or request contents. To find and fix bugs.

We do not sell personal data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics cookies. We do not use workspace content to train AI models.

Legal basis

We follow Serbia's Law on Personal Data Protection and, for people in the European Union, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

We process account data because it is needed to provide Cadence to you (performance of a contract), and technical logs and error reports because we have a legitimate interest in running a secure and reliable service. Workspace content is processed on the instructions of the workspace owner, which is responsible for having a legal basis for it.

Google sign-in

If you choose "Continue with Google", we receive only your basic profile (name, email address, profile picture) through the openid, email and profile permissions, and use it only to sign you in. We do not get access to your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or any other Google data. Cadence's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How long we keep it

  • Account and workspace data: while the account or workspace is in use. When the workspace owner asks us to close it, we delete its content.
  • Backups: up to 90 days, then deleted.
  • Server logs: a few weeks at most, then overwritten.
  • Error reports: up to 90 days.

Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Each workspace's data is separated in the database so one workspace can never read another's. The database is not reachable from the internet, passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and never stored by Cadence, and backup copies are encrypted before they leave our servers.

Your browser

Cadence keeps you signed in with one cookie (crm_session, up to 30 days, removed when you sign out), plus a short-lived cookie while "Continue with Google" runs, and stores which workspace you last opened in your browser's storage, only so the app works. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your personal data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive it in a portable format. Email alex@simplicity-labs.com and we will answer within one month. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Serbia, the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection; in the EU, the authority where you live or work.

Workspace owners and admins can export and delete their workspace's records in the app. To delete your account, email us.

Children

Cadence is a business tool and is not meant for anyone under 16.

Changes

When this policy changes, we update the date above. For important changes, we will tell workspace owners by email or in the app before they apply.
